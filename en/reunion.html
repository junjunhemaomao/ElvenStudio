ANNUAL REUNION

Annual Reunion

Elven Studio was dissolved as a company years ago, but most of the people who worked here together have stayed in games — and most of us are still in Chengdu.

Time has moved on, and we have scattered into different companies and roles, but Elven Studio remains the place our memories return to. For many of us it was our first job in games, and it holds a youth we can never go back to.

Most of us were born in the 1980s, and the older we get, the more we value the years we spent growing up together. So we meet once a year to catch up. Here is the record, year by year.
