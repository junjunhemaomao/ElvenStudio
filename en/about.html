About Us

The Studio

Elven Studio is an outsourcing company located in Chengdu, the economic and cultural center of south-west of China. We provide scaled production services to the global game industry. The team is composed of energetic and creative veterans. We have years of experiences in not only video game computer graphics creation but also TV series and CG feature movie production, which provided profound artistic background so we can work to different styles and technical requirements for clients home and abroad. Our resources enable us to provide reliable quality with very competitive prices.
